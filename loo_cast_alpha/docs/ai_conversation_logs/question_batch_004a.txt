Current raw pressure from batch 004:

- Phase 2 is no-coding planning/glossary/RFC/spec crystallization for Phase 3 and Phase 4.
- Phase 3 is not a small MVP. It is a feature-complete-enough Vapor/launcher/modpack/capability proof.
- Phase 3 includes real launcher, Steam/Workshop integration, modpack composition, Rhai, capabilities, diagnostics, and
  hello-world-on-steroids output.
- Phase 3 excludes USF, worldmodel, rendering, save/load, chunks, metrics, phenomena, zones, and traditional
  gameplay/simulation except the string/log proof.
- Phase 4 is Phase 3 plus a full working USF stack/prototype/MVP.
- The word `package` is weak/overloaded and should probably be replaced or sharply scoped.
- The largest current risk is uncontrolled Phase 3 feature creep caused by "full Vapor" being underspecified.
