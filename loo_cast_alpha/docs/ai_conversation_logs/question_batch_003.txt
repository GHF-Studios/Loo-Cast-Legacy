Capability Bedrock

But, non.circular "dependencies" are what actualla kinda form (part of) the capability graph, yk? I hope that framing helps.

But even then, I don't think anonymity is a good idea.... no, don't allow anonymous capabilities, but private/internal as opposed to public is okay. Not EVERY little thing, but the larger structure and architecture or whatever, ofc, yk? It's kinda just not as blck-or-white as your question made this out to be, yk?

Just, one type per slot, and a node cannot have itself as a slot type, neither as a dependency nor as a dependant.
