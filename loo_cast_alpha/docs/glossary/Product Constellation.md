Product Constellation is the system-level product relationship model that treats ecosystem, engine product, game
product, and creator surfaces as one coherent compositional product space rather than isolated products.
At this layer, [[Vapor Ecosystem]] is the Steam-specific ecosystem/SDK/distribution context, [[Spacetime Engine]] is
the first-party engine/framework product, and [[Loo Cast]] is the first-party playable game/content product.
[[USF]] is not a product pillar here; it is a pivotal module/framework part inside the Spacetime Engine that Loo Cast
uses.
The strategic product direction is that these remain mutually reinforcing along the [[Player-to-Creator Path]] under
the commitments of [[Project Ethos]].
Role-level dependency shape across these pillars is tracked in [[Pillar Dependency Topology]].

Naming caveat:
`Loo Cast` may also refer informally to the flagship product bundle acquired by a player, where the game grants access
to the Spacetime Engine and Vapor-facing SDK/modding rails.
When precision matters, distinguish the [[Loo Cast]] game from the broader product stack.
