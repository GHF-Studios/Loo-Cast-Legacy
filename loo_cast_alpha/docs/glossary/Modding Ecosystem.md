The Modding Ecosystem is the creator-facing layer for publishing, composing, and evolving content through the [[SDK]]
and the [[Contract]].
It operationalizes creator agency around the runtime stack and extends the same [[Player-to-Creator Path]] beyond
gameplay.

Current naming direction:
When referring to the Steam-specific SDK/distribution/identity ecosystem above concrete engines, games, and mods, use
[[Vapor Ecosystem]].
The modding ecosystem of the Spacetime Engine and Loo Cast is an official use/exposure of that broader ecosystem layer,
not necessarily the whole ecosystem by itself.
